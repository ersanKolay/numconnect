Num Connect

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026 (version 11)

Summary

Num Connect (on the App Store previously listed as "Puzzle Master") is a free number-path puzzle game published by Ersan Kolay. You play without signing in, and we never ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.

To check your solutions, show you how fast you solved a level compared with other players, tune the difficulty of the levels, find bugs and show ads, the app sends some technical information to our own game server and to Google AdMob. This page explains exactly what, why and who receives it.

We never ask for and never collect: your name, e-mail address, phone number, postal address, payment details, precise location, contacts, photos or health data.

Our game server is operated by Ersan Kolay on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure; connections to it pass through Cloudflare. Both act only as infrastructure providers on our behalf.

Advertising and your choices

The game is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob. You choose to watch a short rewarded ad to unlock the hints of a level; the first 10 levels have free hints. The game may also show a banner at the bottom of the play screen.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

Before any ad is requested you are shown a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform, and you decide whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change that choice at any time with Privacy options on the app's home screen.

On iPhone and iPad

iOS asks you separately whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and you see non-personalised ads. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Non-personalised ads still require basic data such as your approximate region and the ad shown, in order to display an ad and prevent fraud.

Who else receives your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers below:

  • Google (AdMob) — advertising. How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Cloudflare — hosting of and network access to our game server.

How long we keep it

Your anonymous player ID, solve times and statistics are kept while your anonymous account exists. Usage and diagnostic events are kept for at most 24 months and then deleted. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent.

Delete my data on the app's home screen immediately deletes your anonymous account, your solve times, statistics and events from our server; the app then continues with a new anonymous ID. You can also e-mail ersankolay@gmail.com and we will help within 30 days. Advertising consent can be changed at any time with Privacy options.

Earlier versions

Versions before 11.0 used Google Firebase (authentication, database and analytics) for leaderboards and duels. Version 11 no longer uses Firebase, leaderboards or duels; the old Firebase project will be shut down and its data deleted.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Our game server is not directly reachable from the internet; it is only reachable through an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel, and each player can read and change only their own records.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

Özet

Num Connect (App Store'da önceden "Puzzle Master" adıyla listelenirdi), Ersan Kolay'ın yayınladığı ücretsiz bir sayı yolu bulmaca oyunudur. Giriş yapmadan oynarsınız; adınızı, e-posta adresinizi ya da telefon numaranızı asla sormayız.

Bu sayfa neyin, neden toplandığını ve kimin aldığını açıklar.

Hiçbir zaman istemediğimiz ve toplamadığımız bilgiler: adınız, e-posta adresiniz, telefon numaranız, posta adresiniz, ödeme bilgileriniz, kesin konumunuz, rehberiniz, fotoğraflarınız ve sağlık verileriniz.

Reklamlar ve seçimleriniz

Oyun ücretsizdir ve Google AdMob reklamlarıyla finanse edilir. Bir bölümün ipuçlarını açmak için kısa bir ödüllü reklamı izlemeyi siz seçersiniz; ilk 10 bölümde ipuçları ücretsizdir. Oyun ekranının altında bir banner reklam da gösterilebilir.

Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de

Herhangi bir reklam istenmeden önce Google'ın Kullanıcı Mesajlaşma Platformu'nun onay formu gösterilir ve verilerinizin kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlar için kullanılıp kullanılmayacağına siz karar verirsiniz. Bu seçimi istediğiniz zaman uygulamanın ana sayfasındaki Gizlilik seçenekleri ile değiştirebilirsiniz.

Android'de
